Operating Partner

An adaptive method for turning objectives into results.

V2ADV starts by understanding the organization: its objective, its current position, and what is or isn't working today. From there, V2ADV organizes the plan and coordinates the right mix of intelligent tools toward the goal. Five core tenets guide every engagement: understanding, organizing, executing, learning, and improving.

  1. UnderstandClarify the objective and current position.
  2. OrganizeBuild the plan around the outcome.
  3. ExecuteCoordinate the systems and work required.
  4. LearnMeasure results and interpret what they show.
  5. ImproveApply what's learned to strengthen what comes next.
