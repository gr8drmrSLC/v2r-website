Start Here

V2ADV models the objective before any work begins.

Every engagement starts with a clear model of the objective, the client's current position, and the path between them. A prospective client can begin that process in one of three ways.

Share the Objective

A structured intake gathers the organization, the objective, and the current starting position. Email contact@v2adv.com to begin. V2ADV is building a self-service version of this same intake.

Talk It Through

A live, real-time conversation that helps a prospective client define the objective in more depth is in development. V2ADV plans to offer it alongside the written intake once it is ready.

Schedule a Call

V2ADV arranges a direct call with the founder by email until its own scheduling system is live. A call beginning more than ten minutes past its set time is canceled and rescheduled.

Value-Based Pricing

Pricing reflects the value of the objective.

V2ADV prices each engagement to the value the objective delivers to the client. Efficient delivery keeps the investment focused directly on the work that reaches that objective. V2ADV presents pricing once it delivers a model of the proposed solution. Every objective breaks into specific goals, each with key performance indicators V2ADV tracks and reports throughout the engagement.
