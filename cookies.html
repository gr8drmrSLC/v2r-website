Cookies Policy

Effective and last updated: September 2026.

What a Cookie Is

A cookie is a small text file a website stores in a visitor's browser to remember information between visits or requests.

What This Site Uses Today

This site is served through Cloudflare, which sets essential cookies needed for security and content delivery, distinguishing legitimate visitors from automated abuse and routing a request to the correct server. This site does not set any first-party analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies, and Cloudflare's essential cookies are not used to track an individual visitor across other sites.

What Is Added Later

If V2ADV adds visitor analytics, to understand which pages are useful rather than to identify an individual, or a live chat or intake tool, this policy names the specific tool, what it sets, and how to opt out, before that tool goes live.

Managing Cookies

Most browsers allow blocking or deleting cookies through their own settings, available in each browser's privacy or security preferences. Blocking essential cookies affects how this site loads or performs.

Changes to This Policy

V2ADV updates this policy as the cookies this site actually uses change. The "last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.
