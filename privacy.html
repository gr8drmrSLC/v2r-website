Privacy Policy

Draft, not yet attorney-reviewed. Last updated: September 2026.

This is a working draft, published ahead of V2ADV collecting any real visitor data, so the policy is in place before it is needed rather than after. The draft has not yet been reviewed by an attorney and will be revised once V2ADV's contact and objective-intake process is actually live.

What this site collects today

As of this policy's last update, this site is a static informational page that does not run a contact form, does not use tracking cookies, and does not collect names, emails, or any other visitor-submitted information. V2ADV's hosting and content-delivery providers record standard web server and content-delivery logs (IP address, browser type, timestamp) as an ordinary part of serving any website; V2ADV does not access these logs to identify individual visitors.

What this site will collect once its intake process is live

V2ADV intends to add a process for a prospective client to describe their objective, so V2ADV can prepare a plan and a quote. Once that process is live, this section will describe exactly what is collected (for example, a name, an email address, and a description of the objective), how it is used (to prepare and follow up on a plan), how long it is kept, and how to request it be deleted. This policy will be updated before that process collects any real submission, not after.

What V2ADV does not do

  • V2ADV does not sell visitor or client information to any third party.
  • V2ADV does not share submitted information with an unrelated third party without the submitter's own consent.
  • V2ADV does not run affiliate links or third-party advertising on this site.

Client data during an engagement

For a client that engages V2ADV, data ownership follows the same principle stated on V2ADV's own homepage. The client owns the business and the assets that define it, including client relationships and client data, as set out in that client's own engagement agreement. This privacy policy governs this website; a signed engagement agreement governs data handling during an actual engagement.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to contact@v2adv.com once it is live. Until then, this policy exists as a public, dated record, not as an active intake channel.
